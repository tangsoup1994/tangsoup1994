# 03 固定搭配

动词与介词的搭配、固定短语。介词是 Part 5 高频考点，重点记「哪个词配哪个介词」。

| 搭配 / 短语 | 含义 | 题中例句（英文原文） | 备注 |
|-------------|------|----------------------|------|
| prior to + 名词 | 在……之前（= before） | ten business days prior to the scheduled session | 介词短语，后接名词；Part 5 常用它替换 before |
| in case / in case of | in case + 句子、in case of + 名词：以防、万一 | —（本题干扰项 A） | 是连词 / 介词短语，义为「以防」，与「之前」无关，别和 prior to 混 |
| rapid expansion | 快速扩张 | has seen rapid expansion in East Asia | 形容词 + 名词搭配；说扩张速度快，不用 bright / loud / proud |
| due to + 名词 | 由于，因为 | due to the popularity of its clothing lines；Due to the heavy rain this morning | `due to` 后接名词或名词短语；如果后面是完整句子才用 because / while 等连词 |
| put + 宾语 + first | 把……放在首位，优先考虑…… | put quality service and customer care first | 商务服务理念常用表达；`first` 表优先级 |
| suggest doing sth | 建议做某事（suggest 后接动名词，不接 to do） | the director suggested filming the scene inside the studio | filming 是 suggest 的动名词宾语 |
| work in the industry | 在该行业工作 | have worked in the industry for many years | `industry` 前用 `the` 指特定行业；现在完成时强调经验延续到现在 |
| heavy rain | 大雨 | the heavy rain this morning | `heavy` 可修饰 rain / traffic / workload 等，表示程度大 |
| experience delays | 出现 / 遭遇延误 | the city’s subway system is experiencing delays | 交通、物流、项目进度场景常用；主语通常是系统、航班、服务或项目 |
| so + adj. + that + 句子 | 如此……以至于…… | so powerful that it can cut sheets of metal | `so` 修饰形容词 / 副词，`that` 后接结果句 |
| up to + 数量 | 多达，最高达到 | up to one centimeter thick | 用于说明上限、规格或能力范围 |
| financial specialist | 财务专员 / 财务专家 | financial specialist, Richard Bigsby | `financial` 修饰表示人的职位名词 `specialist` |
| handle budgetary decisions | 处理预算决策 | handles all budgetary decisions | `handle` 可表示负责处理事务、决策、问题 |
| be excited to announce that + 句子 | 很高兴宣布…… | is excited to announce that this month’s guest speaker is Joshua Ramsey | 公告、活动通知常用开头，`that` 后接完整句子 |
| expert in + 领域 | 某领域的专家 | an expert in climate science | `expert` 指人；`in` 后接专业领域 |
| environmental agency | 环保机构，环境机构 | a new environmental agency | `environmental` 修饰机构类型 |
| be established to do sth | 被成立来做某事 | a new environmental agency established to address the city’s pollution problem | 常用过去分词 `established` 后接不定式表目的 |
| address a problem | 处理 / 解决问题 | address the city’s pollution problem | `address` 在商务和公共事务语境中常表示着手处理问题 |
| be expected to do sth | 被期望 / 应该做某事 | Each employee is expected to use their designated parking space | 规章、通知、岗位要求中常用，语气比 must 稍缓 |
| designated parking space | 指定停车位 | their designated parking space | `designated` 作形容词，说明停车位已被分配或指定 |
| upward trend in sales | 销售额上升趋势 | The current upward trend in sales | `in sales` 说明趋势发生在销售数据上 |
| be likely to do sth | 很可能做某事 | is likely to continue through the upcoming holiday season | `likely` 后接不定式，表示可能性 |
| continue through + 时间段 | 持续贯穿某段时间 | continue through the upcoming holiday season | `through` 接时间段，强调一直持续到该期间结束 |
| be praised for + 名词 / 名词短语 | 因……受到称赞 | have been praised for the particularly soft materials they use | `for` 后接受到称赞的原因 |
| particularly soft materials | 特别柔软的材料 | particularly soft materials they use | `particularly` 修饰形容词 `soft`，`soft` 再修饰名词 `materials` |
| under construction | 正在建设中 / 施工中 | is currently under construction | `under + 名词` 表状态；建筑、道路、设施项目常用 `construction` |
| temporarily suspended | 暂时暂停 | will be temporarily suspended | 公共服务、交通、活动通知中常用；强调暂停是临时的 |
| while repairs are being made | 在维修进行期间 | while repairs are being made | `while` 引导时间状语从句，`are being made` 表维修正在进行 |
| praise sb for doing sth | 因做某事称赞某人 | praised Ms. Fong for producing such an innovative advertising campaign | `for` 后接名词或动名词，说明称赞原因 |
| innovative advertising campaign | 创新的广告活动 | an innovative advertising campaign | 形容词 `innovative` 修饰营销活动 |
| line of + 产品 | ……产品线 / 系列产品 | the new line of televisions | 商务语境中 `line` 常指一组同类产品 |
| many opportunities to do sth | 许多做某事的机会 | many opportunities to sample products and learn how they are made | `opportunities` 是复数可数名词，前面直接用 `many` |
| sample products | 试吃 / 试用产品 | sample products | 展会、门店推广和产品发布场景常用 |
| learn how + 句子 | 了解如何…… | learn how they are made | `how` 后接完整句子，说明要了解的制作方式 |
| be regarded as | 被视为，被认为是 | is regarded as one of the most influential modern authors | 被动结构；主语是被评价的人或事物 |
| one of the most + 形容词 + 复数名词 | 最……的……之一 | one of the most influential modern authors | `one of` 后面的名词要用复数 |
| participate in | 参与，参加 | was already participating in Bigmart’s same-day delivery program | 后接活动、计划、项目等名词 |
| same-day delivery program | 当日配送计划 | Bigmart’s same-day delivery program | `delivery` 是名词作定语，说明计划类型 |
| select markets | 部分选定市场 | began in select markets last fall | `select` 作形容词，表示经过选择的、精选的 |
| method for doing sth | 做某事的方法 | a cost-effective method for harnessing solar energy | `for` 后接动名词，说明方法用途 |
| harness solar energy | 利用太阳能 | harnessing solar energy | 能源技术语境常用 `harness` 表开发利用 |
| be agreed upon | 被同意 / 达成一致 | have been formally agreed upon | 被动结构；常用于协议、政策、变更等已经达成一致 |
| be effective until + 时间 | 到……才生效 / 有效至…… | will not be effective until next month | 否定句中 `not ... until` 表「直到……才……」 |
| grow by + 百分比 | 增长了…… | grew by 26 percent last year | `by` 后接增长幅度 |
| surpass forecasts | 超过预测 | surpassed even the most optimistic forecasts | 表实际结果超过预期或预测 |
| the most optimistic forecasts | 最乐观的预测 | the most optimistic forecasts | `optimistic` 修饰复数名词 `forecasts` |
| rehearse a presentation | 排练演示 / 发表 | rehearsed his presentation | 正式汇报、会议发言或销售展示前常用 |
| in front of + 人 | 在某人面前 | in front of his colleagues | 说明动作发生在听众或观察者面前 |
| receive feedback | 获得反馈 | to receive feedback | `feedback` 常作不可数名词，动词可用 receive / get / provide / give |
| enthusiasm for + 名词 | 对……的热情 | Ms. Cho’s enthusiasm for oil painting | `for` 后接热情所指向的对象；不要误用表示人的 `enthusiast` |
