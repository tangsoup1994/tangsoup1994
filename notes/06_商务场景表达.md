# 06 商务场景表达

邮件、会议、合同等商务场景的高频表达，按场景归类，方便按情境复习。

| 场景 | 表达（英文原文） | 含义 / 用法 | 题中例句 |
|------|------------------|-------------|----------|
| 客户服务 | put quality service and customer care first | 把优质服务和客户关怀放在首位，常用于公司服务理念或品牌承诺 | Here at Auto Empire, we always put quality service and customer care first. |
| 零售门店 | retail locations | 零售点、门店、线下销售地点；公司谈门店规模或网点分布时常用 | all the company’s retail locations |
| 教育培训 | active professionals | 仍在行业中活跃工作的专业人士，常用于说明教师、讲师或顾问具备实务经验 | are active professionals who have worked in the industry for many years |
| 交通通知 | subway system is experiencing delays | 地铁系统正在出现延误，常用于公共交通、通勤或运营通知 | the city’s subway system is experiencing delays |
| 产品性能 | can cut sheets of metal up to one centimeter thick | 能切割厚达一厘米的金属板，用于说明工具、机器或设备性能 | it can cut sheets of metal up to one centimeter thick |
| 财务职责 | handles all budgetary decisions | 负责处理所有预算决策，常用于说明财务、管理岗位职责 | Richard Bigsby, handles all budgetary decisions |
| 活动通知 | is excited to announce that this month’s guest speaker is ... | 很高兴宣布本月嘉宾演讲者是……，用于学校、会议或活动公告 | is excited to announce that this month’s guest speaker is Joshua Ramsey |
| 环境治理 | address the city’s pollution problem | 处理 / 解决城市污染问题，常用于政府、机构或环保项目说明 | established to address the city’s pollution problem |
| 员工规定 | use their designated parking space | 使用自己的指定停车位，常用于公司内部规定或设施使用说明 | Each employee is expected to use their designated parking space |
| 销售趋势 | upward trend in sales is likely to continue | 销售额上升趋势很可能继续，用于销售报告、业绩预测或市场分析 | The current upward trend in sales is likely to continue through the upcoming holiday season |
| 产品材料评价 | be praised for the particularly soft materials | 因材料特别柔软而受到称赞，常用于产品评价、宣传或用户反馈 | Jackson Bedding’s mattress lines have been praised for the particularly soft materials they use |
| 商场扩建 | a new annex is currently under construction | 新附属建筑正在建设中，常用于商业地产、设施扩建或施工公告 | A new annex, which will expand the Bayview Mall, is currently under construction |
| 交通维修通知 | train service will be temporarily suspended | 列车服务将暂时暂停，常用于维修、施工或运营调整公告 | Train service to and from the Luzern District will be temporarily suspended while repairs are being made |
| 广告营销 | produce an innovative advertising campaign | 制作创新广告活动，常用于产品上市、品牌推广或营销成果评价 | Ms. Fong for producing such an innovative advertising campaign for the new line of televisions |
| 展会试吃 / 试用 | sample products and learn how they are made | 试吃 / 试用产品并了解制作过程，常用于食品饮料展会、产品展示或品牌推广 | At the Food & Beverage Fair, there will be many opportunities to sample products and learn how they are made |
| 零售配送 | same-day delivery program | 当日配送计划，常用于零售、电商或本地门店配送服务 | Meridian Town was already participating in Bigmart’s same-day delivery program |
| 能源技术 | a cost-effective method for harnessing solar energy | 利用太阳能的高性价比方法，常用于研发、环保技术或能源项目说明 | A researcher from Beckerton University has developed a cost-effective method for harnessing solar energy |
| 政策变更 | changes to the company policy will be effective | 公司政策变更将生效，常用于内部通知、制度更新或合规公告 | Some changes to the company policy have been formally agreed upon, but they will not be effective until next month |
| 行业增长与预测 | growth surpassed even the most optimistic forecasts | 增长超过最乐观预测，常用于行业报告、财务表现或市场分析 | The fertilizer industry grew by 26 percent last year, which surpassed even the most optimistic forecasts |
| 会议演示反馈 | rehearse a presentation in front of colleagues to receive feedback | 在同事面前排练演示以获得反馈，常用于会议准备、培训或销售汇报场景 | Mr. Salvador rehearsed his presentation in front of his colleagues to receive feedback |
| 职务交接 | a managerial role will be taken over by someone | 某个管理职位 / 职责将由某人接手，常用于人事任命、岗位变更或交接通知 | The managerial role will be taken over by Sharon Wells as early as next month |
| 客户接待 | welcome every customer in a courteous manner | 以礼貌的方式欢迎每位顾客，常用于前台、客服、门店或保险服务场景 | The new receptionist at Clarwell Insurance welcomes every customer in a courteous manner |
| 音乐发行 | release her first solo album | 发行她的首张个人专辑，常用于音乐、娱乐新闻或宣传公告 | Samantha Nighting will release her first solo album later this year |
| 天气预测软件 | weather simulation software reliably predicts storms | 天气模拟软件可靠地预测风暴，常用于技术产品说明、气象服务或软件宣传 | Our new weather simulation software reliably predicts storms up to two weeks before they occur |
| 零售营业时间调整 | have extended hours of operation | 延长营业时间，常用于旺季、促销期或客流增加时的门店运营安排 | To handle the additional customers during the summer, our store will have extended hours of operation |
