# 02 高频词汇

单词的词性、释义、来自题目的例句，以及易混词提示。已记过的词只在「易混词 / 提示」或例句列补充，不重复建行。

| 单词 | 词性 | 释义 | 题中例句（英文原文） | 易混词 / 提示 |
|------|------|------|----------------------|----------------|
| business day | n. | 工作日（不含周末与节假日） | at least ten business days prior to the scheduled session | 不是「商务日」；表期限常与 prior to / before / after 搭配 |
| rapid | adj. | 快速的，迅速的 | has seen rapid expansion in East Asia | 常修饰 expansion / growth / change 等表示发展变化的名词；本题不能用 bright |
| expansion | n. | 扩张，扩大，发展 | rapid expansion in East Asia | 商业语境常指市场、业务、门店、地区覆盖的扩大 |
| popularity | n. | 受欢迎，流行 | due to the popularity of its clothing lines | `due to + 名词` 表原因；popularity 说明扩张原因，不决定选 bright |
| consecutive | adj. | 连续的，接连的 | After several consecutive days of delays due to bad weather | 常修饰 days / years / wins 等表数量或时间的名词 |
| delay | n./v. | 延误，推迟 | several consecutive days of delays due to bad weather；the city’s subway system is experiencing delays | 作名词时可数，常用复数 delays 表多处 / 多次延误；作动词时搭配 delay doing |
| professional | n./adj. | 专业人士；专业的 | are active professionals who have worked in the industry for many years | 作名词指有专业技能或从业经验的人；本题用复数 professionals |
| industry | n. | 行业，产业 | worked in the industry for many years | `the industry` 指前文语境中的行业；常与 work in 搭配 |
| saw | n. | 锯 | Our new electric saw is so powerful | `electric saw` 是电锯；不要误看成 see 的过去式 saw |
| powerful | adj. | 强劲的，功率大的 | so powerful that it can cut sheets of metal | 产品性能语境中常形容机器、工具、设备能力强 |
| metal | n. | 金属 | sheets of metal up to one centimeter thick | `sheets of metal` 指金属板 / 金属片 |
| financial | adj. | 财务的，金融的 | The Langdon Company’s financial specialist | 常修饰 report / decision / specialist / department 等财务相关名词 |
| specialist | n. | 专员，专家 | financial specialist, Richard Bigsby | 表示具有专门职责或专业知识的人；词尾 `-ist` 常指人 |
| budgetary | adj. | 预算的 | handles all budgetary decisions | 常修饰 decisions / constraints / planning 等预算相关名词 |
| announce | v. | 宣布，告知 | is excited to announce that this month’s guest speaker is Joshua Ramsey | 后面常接 `that + 句子` 表宣布的内容 |
| guest speaker | n. | 嘉宾演讲者，特邀讲者 | this month’s guest speaker is Joshua Ramsey | 学校、会议、活动通知中的常见表达 |
| expert | n./adj. | 专家；熟练的 | an expert in climate science | 作名词指人，是可数名词；本题受 `an` 限定用单数 |
| expertise | n. | 专业知识，专长 | —（本题干扰项 A） | 不可数抽象名词，不指专家本人；不能写成 `an expertise in ...` |
| environmental | adj. | 环境的，环保的 | a new environmental agency | 常修饰 agency / policy / issue / impact 等环境相关名词 |
| agency | n. | 机构，部门 | a new environmental agency established to address the city’s pollution problem | 可指政府、公共或专业机构 |
| establish | v. | 成立，建立 | a new environmental agency established to address the city’s pollution problem | 本题用过去分词 `established` 作后置定语，表示被成立 |
| pollution | n. | 污染 | the city’s pollution problem | 不可数名词；作定语时可用于 `pollution problem` |
| employee | n. | 员工，雇员 | Each employee is expected to use their designated parking space | 指受雇于公司的人员，区别于 employer「雇主」 |
| designate | v. | 指定，选定 | their designated parking space | 本题用过去分词 `designated` 作形容词，表示「被指定的」 |
| parking space | n. | 停车位 | designated parking space | 也可说 parking spot；公司场景常涉及员工停车位 |
| trend | n. | 趋势，走向 | The current upward trend in sales | 常与 upward / downward / current 搭配，表示数据变化方向 |
| upward | adj. | 向上的，上升的 | upward trend in sales | 常修饰 trend / movement，表示数据或数量上升 |
| sales | n. | 销售额，销售 | upward trend in sales | 商务语境中常指销售额或销售表现 |
| upcoming | adj. | 即将到来的 | the upcoming holiday season | 常修饰 event / meeting / season / launch 等 |
| mattress | n. | 床垫 | Jackson Bedding’s mattress lines | `mattress lines` 指床垫产品线，`line` 在商务语境中可指产品系列 |
| material | n. | 材料，原料 | particularly soft materials they use | 复数 `materials` 常指产品使用的多种材料或原料 |
| soft | adj. | 柔软的 | particularly soft materials | 形容词修饰名词；前面可由程度副词 `particularly` 加强 |
| particularly | adv. | 尤其，特别 | particularly soft materials | 副词修饰形容词 `soft`，不能直接修饰名词 `materials` |
| annex | n. | 附属建筑，附加部分 | A new annex, which will expand the Bayview Mall | 商业建筑语境中常指为扩大原有设施而新增的附属楼或扩建部分 |
| expand | v. | 扩大，扩建 | which will expand the Bayview Mall | 可指业务、市场、建筑空间等扩大；本题指商场扩建 |
| construction | n. | 建设，施工 | currently under construction | 常用于固定表达 `under construction`，表示正在建设中 |
| train service | n. | 列车服务 | Train service to and from the Luzern District | 交通公告中常说明服务范围、暂停、延误或恢复 |
| suspend | v. | 暂停，中止 | will be temporarily suspended | 常用被动 `be suspended` 表服务、活动、运营被暂停 |
| temporarily | adv. | 暂时地 | temporarily suspended | 修饰 `suspended`，说明暂停是临时状态，不是永久取消 |
| repair | n./v. | 维修，修理 | while repairs are being made | 名词常用复数 `repairs` 表维修工作；`repairs are being made` 表维修正在进行 |
| praise | v. | 称赞，表扬 | praised Ms. Fong for producing such an innovative advertising campaign | 常用 `praise sb for doing sth` 表因某事称赞某人 |
| produce | v. | 制作，产出 | producing such an innovative advertising campaign | 可指制作广告、报告、产品或内容 |
| innovative | adj. | 创新的 | such an innovative advertising campaign | 形容词修饰名词；词尾 `-ive` 常构成形容词 |
| advertising campaign | n. | 广告活动，广告宣传 | an innovative advertising campaign for the new line of televisions | 营销语境常指围绕某产品或品牌的一系列广告活动 |
| fair | n. | 展会，博览会 | At the Food & Beverage Fair | 商务场景中常指行业展会或商品展示活动 |
| opportunity | n. | 机会 | many opportunities to sample products | 可数名词；复数前可用 `many`，不能用 `every` |
| sample | v. | 试吃，试用，体验样品 | sample products | 展会、食品饮料、零售推广语境中常表示试用或试吃产品 |
| product | n. | 产品 | sample products | 商务和营销语境高频词；复数 `products` 表多种产品 |
| regard | v. | 认为，看作 | is regarded as one of the most influential modern authors | 常用被动 `be regarded as` 表「被视为」 |
| influential | adj. | 有影响力的 | one of the most influential modern authors | 常修饰 person / author / figure / leader 等，表示影响力大 |
| author | n. | 作者，作家 | modern authors | 可数名词；`one of ...` 后接复数名词 |
| delivery | n. | 配送，递送 | same-day delivery program | 名词作定语修饰 `program`，表示计划类型 |
| program | n. | 计划，项目 | Bigmart’s same-day delivery program | 商务语境中可指公司推出的服务项目、计划或活动 |
| market | n. | 市场，地区市场 | select markets last fall | `select markets` 指经过选择的部分市场 / 地区 |
| researcher | n. | 研究人员 | A researcher from Beckerton University | 指从事研究的人；常与 university / institute 搭配 |
| cost-effective | adj. | 成本效益高的，高性价比的 | a cost-effective method for harnessing solar energy | 常修饰 method / solution / approach / strategy |
| method | n. | 方法 | a cost-effective method for harnessing solar energy | 常接 `for doing sth` 表做某事的方法 |
| harness | v. | 利用，开发利用 | harnessing solar energy | 常用于能源语境，表示把自然资源转化为可用能源 |
| solar energy | n. | 太阳能 | harnessing solar energy | 可作能源技术、环保项目语境表达 |
| policy | n. | 政策，规定 | changes to the company policy | 公司制度、政策变更语境常用 |
| formally | adv. | 正式地 | have been formally agreed upon | 修饰动作，表示通过正式程序或方式 |
| effective | adj. | 生效的；有效的 | will not be effective until next month | 作表语时可表示规定、政策、变更开始生效 |
| fertilizer | n. | 肥料 | The fertilizer industry | 农业、化工行业语境常用 |
| surpass | v. | 超过，胜过 | surpassed even the most optimistic forecasts | 可接 forecast / expectation / target 等，表示超过预期或预测 |
| forecast | n./v. | 预测，预报 | the most optimistic forecasts | 作名词时可数；商业、行业、天气语境常用 |
| optimistic | adj. | 乐观的 | the most optimistic forecasts | 形容词修饰预测、估计、观点等 |
| rehearse | v. | 排练，预演 | rehearsed his presentation | 后接演示、演讲、表演等内容；商务场景常指正式发表前预演 |
| presentation | n. | 演示，发表，报告 | rehearsed his presentation | 可数名词；会议、培训、销售场景中常指面向听众的正式说明 |
| colleague | n. | 同事 | in front of his colleagues | 可数名词；强调一起工作的人，不等同于 friend |
| feedback | n. | 反馈，意见 | to receive feedback | 不可数名词；常和 receive / give / provide 搭配 |
| enthusiasm | n. | 热情，热忱 | Ms. Cho’s enthusiasm for oil painting | 抽象名词；常和 `for` 搭配，表示对某事的热情 |
| enthusiastic | adj. | 热情的，热衷的 | —（本题干扰项 A） | 形容词常用于 `be enthusiastic about ...`，不能直接作所有格后的中心名词 |
| enthusiast | n. | 热衷者，爱好者 | —（本题干扰项 C） | 指人；不等于抽象名词「热情」 |
| oil painting | n. | 油画 | for oil painting | 可指油画这种艺术形式，也可指一幅油画 |
| artwork | n. | 艺术作品 | local artwork | 集合意义时常作不可数名词，表示艺术作品整体 |
